# Manic Flash Option: security checklist

Each pitfall that matters for this contract is mapped to the design rule that answers it, where that rule lives in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), and the test that proves it. The IDs group the pitfalls by area: AC access control, UPG upgrades, SIG signatures, TOK the token, RE reentrancy, MATH arithmetic, DOS denial of service, ORA the price oracle, L2 the chain, MEV ordering, CMP the compiler, TEST the test suite.

Status: v1.0.0. Each row names the tests that prove it. A row whose answer lies outside the contract (a runbook, monitoring, the audit scope) says so.

---

## The three things most likely to lose money here

1. **The relayer key is our oracle (AC-2, ORA-1).**
   - Ostium (Arbitrum, Jul 2026, ~$18M) lost its pool when a stolen price key opened at fake prices and closed at real ones.
   - Anyone can make user wallets and sign their own orders, so a stolen OPENER or SETTLER key could do the same to us, up to the pool's free money.
   - Answers:
     - Separate opener and settler keys.
     - The guardian can halt and revoke a key at once.
     - The payout circuit breaker (ARCHITECTURE §8.11, ADR 0003).
     - 24/7 monitoring of net payout.
     - Bounded config.
2. **Admin keys (AC-1, AC-3, AC-4).** Bybit (~$1.5B) and Radiant (~$50M) were multisig signers approving a malicious transaction.
   - Answers:
     - Everything that moves money or changes code sits behind a 48 h timelock.
     - Hardware-wallet signers who check the transaction hash on a second device.
     - A Safe guard that blocks `DELEGATECALL`.
     - No role outside the timelock can redirect funds.
3. **The token is not a plain ERC-20 (TOK-2, TOK-3, TOK-4).**
   - USDG on Robinhood Chain is Paxos's facet token. Paxos can swap any function, freeze any address (ours included), pause everything, and wipe frozen balances.
   - Answers:
     - Pull-payment fallback.
     - A balance-delta check on every pull.
     - Internal accounting only, never `balanceOf` for logic.
     - Monitor token upgrades and facet changes.
     - Freezing or wiping *our* address is an accepted risk we can't defend against in code.

## Monitoring (from day one on testnet)

- **Net payout per hour per market.** Alert when it passes 50% of the circuit-breaker limit.
- **Any role change, upgrade proposal, `PayoutDeferred`, `HaltChanged`, or `CircuitBreakerTripped`.** Page someone.
- **Token behaviour:**
  - USDG implementation and facet changes (`getFacet` for transfer, permit, 3009, freeze);
  - pause state;
  - whether our address is frozen.
- **Relayer ETH balance and nonce lag** per key.
- **The gap between `block.timestamp` and `publishTime`** on opens (p50/p99), to tune `entryMaxAgeSecs` and `clockSkewSecs`.
